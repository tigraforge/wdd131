Site Name

CampusNest

CampusNest is a university student information hub designed to help students find important academic, campus, and class information in one organized place.

I chose the name CampusNest because it combines the idea of a university campus with the word "nest." A nest represents a central and comfortable place where students can find useful information, resources, and connections.

Site Purpose

CampusNest will provide university students with organized access to academic resources, campus updates, class information, student communities, and useful daily information.

The website will bring information that is often scattered across different sources into one simple and organized student-focused experience.

The site will help students keep track of their academic responsibilities while making it easier to find useful information about their campus and student community.

Target Audience

The primary audience is university students who need a simple way to keep track of classes, deadlines, announcements, academic resources, and campus information.

Scenarios

  • When is my next class and what important school information do I need to know today?
  • Where can I find materials and past questions for my course?
  • What does this school announcement mean and what do I need to do?

Color Schema

CampusNest will use a clean and modern color scheme that gives the website a friendly and professional student-technology appearance.

Teal

Soft White

#F8FAFC

Used for the main page background.

White

#FFFFFF

Used for cards and information sections.

Typography

CampusNest will use two modern sans-serif fonts to create a clean, readable, and professional design.

Poppins

Poppins will be used for the site name, headings, navigation, and other important labels.

Inter

Inter will be used for body text, descriptions, labels, and general content.
